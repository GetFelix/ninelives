# Security

Please report vulnerabilities privately, through GitHub's
[private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/GetFelix/ninelives/security/advisories/new)
for this repository. Do not open a public issue.

Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and what an attacker gains.
We acknowledge a report within three working days and agree on a
disclosure date with you once the fix is understood.

NineLives has not published an image yet. Reports about the design and
the build pipeline are welcome now.

Vulnerabilities in Felix or felixd belong in their own repositories
([Felix](https://github.com/GetFelix/felix/security),
[felixd](https://github.com/GetFelix/felixd/security)).
